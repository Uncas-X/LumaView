LumaView includes FFmpeg 9.0.2 / ffprobe (LGPL 2.1 or later),
and dav1d 1.5.3 (BSD 2-Clause). Their license texts are in this directory.
FFmpeg and dav1d are unmodified. No GPL or nonfree build options are enabled.
Official projects: https://ffmpeg.org/ and https://code.videolan.org/videolan/dav1d

The accompanying LumaView-source.zip contains the exact source archives under
LumaView/ThirdParty/sources and the reproducible configuration/build script
LumaView/tools/build-media-tools.sh. The app invokes these as separate programs.
Modified tools may be substituted and the local app re-signed using build.sh.
